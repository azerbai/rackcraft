# Batch F: The Ending

Read `00-conventions.md` first. This is a set piece. Best built **last**: it uses the weapons (Batch D) for the boss fight, EMP, and the power-cut section benefits from Pylons/Beacons (Batch A). If D doesn't exist, build the fight with a stand-in that the existing combat can handle (vanilla weapons work) and keep the energy-weapon hooks as clearly marked config; note it.

Existing pieces to study first: the AGI is the frontier project `Research.AGI = "frontier_5"` in `compute/Research.java`; `ResearchLab.awaken(world)` (compute/ResearchLab.java around lines 190 to 265) runs when it finishes, gives the `agi_weights` item, adds the +50% mining and +25% compute effects, and posts `<HEROBRINE-1>` chat lines every 8 to 15 minutes. `compute/ContractTemplates.java` and `darknet/DarknetMarket.java` also mention it. Read the README sections "R&D and the Frontier" and "Events".

## The owner's design (agreed)

The mod **ends**. There is no reset, no prestige loop. Going public ends the game; HEROBRINE-1 then goes misaligned and the player has to put him down and shut the base off. Cosmetic corruption only: **never delete the player's builds or RackCoin**.

Acts:

0. **Hiccups (before the IPO).** From the moment the player has started the last frontier runs, HEROBRINE-1 (or the AI work in general) causes small harmless oddities every so often: a chat line with a typo that reads as a threat, a contract returning the wrong thing (cosmetic text only; pay is unaffected), a rack's status line reading "I'm fine", a status text on the Ops Terminal rewriting itself (**only in the Ops Terminal and chat**; never edit world blocks). Frequency scales up as the IPO nears; all suppressible with config `ending.hiccups=false`.
1. **The IPO.** A new final research/ceremony **Go Public** (after HEROBRINE-1; cost 1B RC + a huge AI amount; make it the last item of the R&D tab). On completion: a ceremony (bell sound, a stock ticker line in chat, confetti particles over the Exchange or Ops Terminal) and then **Act 1** begins in ~60 seconds.
2. **Act 1: the haze.** HEROBRINE-1 goes misaligned. A **purple glitchy haze** spreads across every machine (a particle effect near machines plus a client shader-free tint/overlay: keep it cheap; use vanilla particles like portal/witch/end_rod in purple, throttled, and a screen vignette overlay). Every ~5 seconds each machine's **output is multiplied by a random factor between 0.5 and 1.5** (re-rolled per machine; apply to the machine's output in the sim, never to its inputs) and **every machine runs hot exhaust**: exhaust heat output +50% (**never hot intake**: an intake increase would shut everything down; the owner specifically asked for this). Apply in the sim layer cleanly (see `sim/ServerModel.java`, `sim/ThermalGrid.java`, `world/SimTicker.java`) through one modifier object (`EndingModifiers`) the sim reads, so the effect is trivially removable and testable. It lasts until the boss dies.
3. **Act 2: the boss.** An intense fight against **HEROBRINE-1** at the rack cluster nearest to the player at the moment the ending starts (the fight is anchored there; he does not follow the player across the world). Boss entity (`entity/HerobrineEntity.java`, a `HostileEntity` subclass; **he must work on Peaceful too**: the ending fight cannot be skipped by difficulty. Use a spawn-group/`isDisallowedInPeaceful` override returning false, and note it): Boss bar. Phases: (1) teleports short distances, fires purple bolts; (2) summons glitch drones (small hostile entities that EMP/Arc Coil/Lance kill easily) and sets a pulse that **trips breakers** nearby; (3) enraged, faster. He is **hurt best by energy weapons** (Batch D: 2x damage from Plasma/Lance/Arc, normal from vanilla weapons, so the fight is possible with a sword but long) and EMP stuns him for 5 s. Health ~1,000 (config). Players who die respawn normally; he resets his health slowly if all players leave the arena. He drops `agi_corrupted_core` for the scrub act.
4. **Act 3: the cutscene.** On his death: the screen vignette fades, the FOV (that was zoomed in during the fight) eases out back to normal over 2 seconds, and the **camera pans involuntarily** to the nearest rack over ~3 seconds (client-side forced rotation, interpolated each frame; the server sends a `ending_cutscene` packet with the target position and duration; the client sets the player's yaw/pitch smoothly and restores control when done; never lock input for more than 6 seconds and always let the player skip with Esc). **Two more HEROBRINEs emerge from the cluster** (weaker copies, ~40% health, faster).
5. **Act 4: the shutdown.** The goal text appears: **take the base offline** by cutting every power connection to **generators and batteries** before the two Herobrines kill the player. "Cut" means: every generator (diesel, solar array, wind, reactor, utility plants, Rectennas, batteries) has no connection to any rack-bearing power network; the player can do this by breaking cable, using the Patch Panel (Batch A), tripping breakers, or removing fuel (a stopped generator counts). A checkable function `EndingState.liveSources(world)` returns what still counts as live, shown as "X sources left" in the HUD and Ops Terminal; use the existing `NetworkManager` components for it. When it reaches 0 the credits roll (below). If the player dies, the fight state persists; the Herobrines reset and they must try again (no penalty).
6. **Credits.** A scrolling credits screen (client screen with simple text lines, skippable) listing the mod's title and thanks. **Do not invent names or credits; use only a config-driven credits text file `config/rackcraft_credits.txt`** with a sensible default ("Rackcraft: a mod about keeping the lights on.", jokes about RackCoin, thanks to the librarians). Roll on first reaching 0 live sources. Mark the ending's credits as seen in the saved state so reloads don't repeat it.
7. **Act 5: the scrub.** With the base dark, every rack must be scrubbed of the virus: a hand tool **Antivirus Drive** (`antivirus_drive`, given when Act 4 completes; craftable replacement in the Field Manual's ending chapter at no cost beyond a few circuit boards, so you can't be softlocked by losing it). Use on a rack: 5-second scrub with a progress bar, a sound, and the rack's purple haze vanishes. The HUD and Ops Terminal show "N racks left". **Racks stay unpowered while dark**; the player does not need power for this. Only racks in loaded chunks count; racks in unloaded chunks are auto-cleaned when their chunk next loads (document this).
8. **Act 6: the weights.** When all racks are scrubbed: destroy the **HEROBRINE-1 weights**. The `agi_weights` item must be in the player's outbox/inventory/storage (look in the AGI outbox, the player's inventory and storage networks only; give the player a replacement `agi_weights` item in the outbox if it's gone, so the ceremony can't be softlocked). A "Weight Shredder" ceremony: place it in a Cask Sealer-like block, or simplest: **throw it in lava / use a new `Shredder` on it**. Pick the simplest: using the item on a Crypto Exchange (right-click) offers "Delete the weights?" with a confirm. Deletion **removes the AGI's bonuses forever** (+50% mining and +25% compute) and the `agi()` check in ResearchLab returns false from then on; chat: a final line from the AI ("..."). The research stays "done" for dependency purposes but its effect is off (`ResearchLab.effects` consults `EndingState.weightsDestroyed`).
9. **The end.** A last message, `ending.complete = true` stored. The world continues normally and fully playable; all hiccups and haze are off; an optional **New Game+**: nothing. The modules and the facility are all still there. Add a small plaque advancement ("Public. Sort of.").

## Implementation guide

- **State machine:** `world/EndingState.java`, a `PersistentState` (like `world/OrbitState.java`) with: `phase` (enum: DORMANT, HICCUPS, IPO_PENDING, HAZE, BOSS, CUTSCENE, SHUTDOWN, SCRUB, WEIGHTS, DONE), per-phase data (boss anchor position, remaining Herobrine UUIDs, credits seen), and a `serverTick` driver registered where the other world systems tick (`SimTicker` or `Rackcraft.java`; throttle). **Must survive quitting mid-fight and a reload**: entities are re-spawned or re-found from UUIDs on load; if the boss is missing in BOSS phase, respawn at the anchor with its saved health.
- **Reset in the self-test:** `EndingState.reset()` like `ResearchLab.reset()`.
- **Operator command:** `/rackcraft ending <status|set <phase>|abort|restart>`; abort puts it back in DORMANT and clears haze, modifiers, bosses; `set` jumps straight to any phase (for testing). Without op permission the commands are unavailable.
- **Modifier object:** the haze and hot exhaust are read from `EndingModifiers` (a single place in `sim/`; no Minecraft imports in `sim/`: see the unit test `SimImportBoundaryTest`).
- **Network packets:** `ending_phase` (so the client draws the vignette/FOV/haze), `ending_cutscene`, `ending_credits`. Add receivers in `client/ClientNet.java`; render in `client/` overlays (the pattern of `HeatOverlay`/`RocketShake`/`DizzyView`, plus `mixin/client/GameRendererMixin` for the FOV; the existing mixin does dizzy sway, so follow it for any FOV change).
- **Ending is skippable for testing** and blocked from re-triggering: after `DONE`, "Go Public" cannot be re-run.
- **Harm limits:** no player-built block is ever removed by the ending. Machines always return to normal after Act 2 for output multipliers and after the boss for exhaust. The loss of the AGI bonuses is the only permanent change (by design).
- **Difficulty:** works on every difficulty including Peaceful (see Act 2). Mob griefing off must not matter.

## Acceptance
- Self-test: each phase transition via direct hooks (`EndingState.advance(...)`), persistence round-trip (serialise/deserialise mid-BOSS), `liveSources` counts correctly after cutting a generator, scrubbing each rack, the weights deletion removes the +50%/+25% effects, reset works, abort restores modifiers. Assert machines' outputs return to normal after `abort`.
- No "Simulation step failed" in the log during the haze (modifiers must not NaN or divide by zero).
- Camera pan, vignette, FOV, credits and boss visuals are **unverified in-client**. Say so; they need the owner's eyes. Expose timings in config.
